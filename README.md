## a little about me

Hey, I'm **Nylo** (or **Nylo Refined** online).

I like being involved in both sides of a project: figuring out how it works and making it feel good to use. That usually means a mix of backend code, interfaces, automation, and a lot of testing things until they actually work.

Most of my ideas start with *"could I build that?"* and go from there. Sometimes it's a practical tool. Sometimes it's a visual experiment, a game system, or something I just wanted to try.

- **What I build:** web apps, APIs, desktop tools, automation, computer-vision projects, and Roblox experiences/plugins.
- **What I care about:** clean interfaces, small details, working features, and not making simple things unnecessarily complicated.
- **Where I'm headed:** deeper into software engineering, backend systems, and game development.

## on the web

Not everything I make ends up in a standalone repository.

- **[CineAmaze](https://cineamaze.vercel.app)** — my movie and TV website, with discovery, actor pages, watch history, and a short-form trailer feed.
- **[Noir Audio](https://noir-audio.vercel.app)** — browser-based audio tools for effects like 8D audio, bass boost, reverb, and more.
- **[TheStuffDept](https://thestuffdept.etsy.com)** — my digital-product shop. Templates, printables, and the design side of what I do.

I also make **Roblox Studio tools and plugins**, including lighting tools, and experiment with game mechanics, UI systems, Discord bots, and small web utilities.
